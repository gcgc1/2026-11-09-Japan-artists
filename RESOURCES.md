# Six Japanese Artists Resources

## Knowledge

### Kusama

- [Yayoi Kusama obituary — The Guardian (27 Aug 2026)](https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2026/aug/27/yayoi-kusama-obituary)
  The best short account of the life and work, with Kusama’s own words on self-obliteration. Use for: biography, the meaning of the dots, the 1960s happenings.
- [An Introduction to Yayoi Kusama — Tate](https://www.tate.org.uk/art/artists/yayoi-kusama-8094/introduction-yayoi-kusama)
  Clear, well-illustrated timeline of the early years and the New York period. Use for: 1929–70, the move to America, the fashion and anti-war work.
- [The Life and Career of Yayoi Kusama: A Timeline — M+](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/magazine/yayoi-kusama-biography-timeline/)
  Year-by-year chronology with excellent archival images. Use for: exact dates, key works, the late-career revival.
- [Yayoi Kusama — The Broad](https://www.thebroad.org/art/yayoi-kusama)
  Short, incisive essay on the Infinity Nets and the mirror rooms. Use for: close reading of the paintings, “performances in paint”.
- [Yayoi Kusama official site](https://yayoi-kusama.jp/en/)
  Primary source for exhibitions and announcements. Use for: current facts, especially since her death in 2026.
- [Yayoi Kusama Museum, Tokyo](https://yayoikusamamuseum.jp/en/)
  The museum’s exhibition schedule and visitor information. Use for: what is on in Tokyo.
- [Matsumoto City Museum of Art](https://matsumoto-artmuse.jp/en/)
  The hometown museum with a large permanent Kusama collection. Use for: trip planning in Nagano.

### Murakami

- [Takashi Murakami — Kaikai Kiki](https://www.kaikaikiki.com/en/artists/takashi_murakami/)
  The artist’s own company biography, with a detailed chronology. Use for: studio system, exhibitions, the Superflat trilogy.
- [Superflat — MOCA](https://www.moca.org/exhibitions/superflat)
  The museum’s account of the 2001 exhibition and Murakami’s theory. Use for: a precise definition of Superflat, and its link to Edo painting and anime.
- [Takashi Murakami — Gagosian](https://gagosian.com/artists/takashi-murakami/)
  Gallery biography and exhibition history. Use for: recent work, Versailles, the Arhat paintings.
- [©Murakami — Brooklyn Museum (2008)](https://www.brooklynmuseum.org/exhibitions/murakami)
  The first major U.S. retrospective; useful on the art/market question. Use for: the commercial turn, Kaikai Kiki, the otaku debate.

### Nara

- [Everything you need to know about Yoshitomo Nara — Christie’s](https://www.christies.com/en/stories/yoshitomo-nara-collecting-guide-aa7bd7944a774805b39fa91a3cded9e7)
  Excellent interview-based guide with the Aomori curator Shigemi Takahashi. Use for: the children, the knife, the Germany years, the market record.
- [Yoshitomo Nara — Aomori Museum of Art](https://www.aomori-museum.jp/en/)
  The museum with more than 170 Nara works and the 8.5 m *Aomori-ken (Aomori Dog)*. Use for: trip planning, the outdoor dog.
- [Yoshitomo Nara Foundation: The Works](https://www.yoshitomonara.org/en/)
  The catalogue raisonné. Use for: verifying titles, dates, and media.
- [Yoshitomo Nara — David Zwirner](https://www.davidzwirner.com/artists/yoshitomo-nara)
  Gallery biography and current exhibitions. Use for: recent paintings, sculptures, and the 2026 New York show.

### Miyake

- [The Concepts and Work of Issey Miyake — Miyake Design Studio](https://mds.isseymiyake.com/im/en/)
  The most detailed official account of the ideas, from “a piece of cloth” to Pleats Please and A-POC. Use for: process, dates, philosophy.
- [Miyake, Kawakubo, and Yamamoto: Japanese Fashion in the Twentieth Century — The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/miyake-kawakubo-and-yamamoto-japanese-fashion-in-the-twentieth-century)
  A scholarly overview that treats the three designers together. Use for: the “Japanese aesthetic” in global fashion, and how they differ.
- [Issey Miyake — Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Issey-Miyake)
  Reliable factual biography. Use for: Hiroshima, Paris, the 1973 debut, the Kyoto Prize.
- [21_21 DESIGN SIGHT](https://www.2121designsight.jp/en/)
  The Tokyo design museum Miyake co-founded and directed. Use for: trip planning, design exhibitions.

### Kawakubo

- [Rei Kawakubo/Comme des Garçons: Art of the In-Between — The Met (2017)](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2017/rei-kawakubo)
  The exhibition that framed her work as a series of “in-betweens”. Use for: the 2017 show, the curatorial argument, gallery views.
- [Rei Kawakubo: reframing fashion — NGV](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/collecting-comme/rei-kawakubo-reframing-fashion/)
  A concise, well-illustrated account of the 1981 debut and the 1997 collection. Use for: the Paris debut, *Body Meets Dress*.
- [Rei Kawakubo — MoMA audio](https://www.moma.org/audio/playlist/43/702)
  Francesca Granata on *Body Meets Dress, Dress Meets Body*. Use for: a close reading of the “lumps and bumps” collection.
- [Comme des Garçons official site](https://www.comme-des-garcons.com/)
  Primary source for current collections and stores. Use for: Dover Street Market, store locations.

### Yamamoto

- [Yohji Yamamoto official site](https://www.yohjiyamamoto.co.jp/)
  Primary source for collections, Y’s, and Y-3. Use for: current work and store information.
- [Notebook on Cities and Clothes — Wim Wenders Stiftung](https://wimwendersstiftung.de/en/film/notebook-on-cities-and-clothes/)
  The foundation’s page on the 1989 film. Use for: the best portrait of Yamamoto’s process.
- [Yohji Yamamoto — V&A search](https://www.vam.ac.uk/search?q=yohji+yamamoto)
  Collection records and fashion-history context. Use for: garments, construction, the black silhouette.
- [The Japanese Revolution in Paris Fashion — Yuniya Kawamura](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/japanese-revolution-in-paris-fashion-9781859737651/)
  Scholarly account of the 1981 Paris debuts. Use for: the fashion-system argument, not biography.

### Cross-cutting

- [The Japanese Revolution in Paris Fashion — Yuniya Kawamura](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/japanese-revolution-in-paris-fashion-9781859737651/)
  The standard academic account of Kawakubo, Yamamoto, and Miyake in Paris. Use for: the “Japanese invasion” narrative and its problems.
- [Tokyo Art Beat](https://www.tokyoartbeat.com/)
  Current exhibition listings. Use for: checking what is on before a trip.
- [Japan Society (New York)](https://japansociety.org/)
  Talks, films, and exhibitions on Japanese art and design. Use for: events and online talks.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/ArtHistory](https://www.reddit.com/r/ArtHistory/)
  Well-moderated, image-friendly. Use for: “what am I looking at?” questions and reading recommendations.
- [r/fashion](https://www.reddit.com/r/fashion/)
  Broad, sometimes noisy. Use for: fashion-history discussion and identification; ignore trend posts.
- [Tokyo Art Beat events](https://www.tokyoartbeat.com/en/events)
  Not a forum, but the comments and listings are useful for seeing what locals are visiting.
- Local: museum curator talks and member previews at the Met, Tate, M+, and the National Art Center, Tokyo.
  Use for: the “wisdom” layer — hearing how specialists argue about attribution, display, and conservation.

## Gaps

- No single book covers all six artists. The closest is a museum survey of post-war Japanese art plus a fashion-history survey.
- Japanese-language sources are richer for Kusama’s and Miyake’s early reception; the English accounts tend to repeat the same few anecdotes.
- Current exhibition dates change; this workspace records a snapshot as of November 2026.
