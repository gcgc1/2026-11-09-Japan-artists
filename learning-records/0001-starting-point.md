# Starting point: names only

The user begins with the six names and little else: no movements, dates, or key works. They are learning for a Japan trip and want to recognise works in person, not to become an art historian. Implications: keep the lessons object-first and place-based, define every term on first use, and do not assume familiarity with Japanese art history or fashion-system vocabulary. The two-hour constraint is real; depth should come from looking at specific works, not from survey coverage.
