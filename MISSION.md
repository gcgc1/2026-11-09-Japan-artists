# Mission: Six Japanese Artists, One Trip

## Why

I am planning a trip to Japan and want to recognise the work of Yayoi Kusama, Takashi Murakami, Yoshitomo Nara, Issey Miyake, Rei Kawakubo, and Yohji Yamamoto in museums, stores, and public spaces — and understand the post-war history that connects them. The goal is not to become an art historian. It is to walk into a room, know what I am looking at, and have something specific to say about it.

## Success looks like

- Name three key works per artist and place them on a 1945–2026 timeline.
- Explain three connections across the six: the post-war generation, the 1981 Paris debuts, and the 1990s–2000s pop/global market.
- Tell an art work from a fashion garment by its context, materials, and function.
- Visit at least three venues (Matsumoto, Naoshima, Aomori, Tokyo, or a museum abroad) with a five-question looking plan.
- Use the Japanese names and terms correctly in conversation.

## Constraints

- Two hours total; seven short lessons of about 17 minutes.
- Beginner: names only at the start.
- Wants images and video; English with Japanese terms.
- Learning happens in this workspace; no graded assignments.

## Out of scope

- Auction investment advice or price prediction.
- A complete history of Japanese art or fashion.
- Other artists, unless they directly explain one of the six.
- Building a collection.
